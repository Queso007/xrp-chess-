How it works

  • Practice games are free — no deposit, no payouts, just chess.
  • vs Computer is free and needs no sign-in — just you against the engine.
  • Both players stake the same amount of XRP to the game's escrow wallet.
  • Win (checkmate, resignation, time): you get your stake back plus 90% of the loser's stake. The operator takes 10%.
  • Draw: both refunded 95%, operator takes 5% each.
  • Abandon the game (disconnect): counts as a forfeit loss — you get nothing, your opponent wins as normal.
  • If both abandon: each refunded 97.5%, operator takes 2.5% each.
  • Stakes are held by the operator's escrow wallet — you trust the operator, this is not trustless.
